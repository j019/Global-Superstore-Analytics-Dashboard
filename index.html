Global Superstore Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Tableau dashboard suite that transforms raw superstore sales data into actionable insights about profit, delays, discounts, and operational performance across markets, managers, and product categories.

Overview

This project delivers a complete business intelligence solution built on the Global Superstore dataset. It features 25+ worksheets, 11 dashboards, and numerous calculated fields that empower stakeholders to:

  • Monitor KPIs (revenue, profit, quantity, delays)
  • Analyze profit ratios & discount effectiveness
  • Identify loss-making segments & delayed orders
  • Drill down by region, manager, category, country
  • Compare annual profit trends (2019–2022)

All dashboards are interactive — filters, highlight actions, and URL actions (e.g., Wikipedia lookup for countries) provide an intuitive exploration experience.

Key Metrics (Calculated Fields)

Profit Status

Labels each row as Profit, Loss, or No Loss No Profit.

Profit Ratio

SUM(Profit) / SUM(Revenue) — overall margin indicator.

Delay Days

DATEDIFF between expected & actual ship dates.

No. of Delayed Orders

Count of orders with Delay Days > 0.

Annual Profit (2019-2022)

Separate fields for year-over-year profit tracking.

Profit Ratio Category

Tier classification: C1 (≥0.25), C2, etc.

Additional metrics: Total Revenue, Total Profit, Total Shipping Cost, Total Quantity, No. of Orders, No. of Customers, No. of Return Orders, Avg Discount, Avg Delay Days.

Interactive Actions

Data Source & Preparation

Name: Orders+ (Global Superstore) — Excel/CSV (standard Superstore dataset).

Key tables: Orders, Returns, People (managers), Shipping costs.

Preprocessing: No external ETL — all transformations (calculated fields, aliases, hierarchies) are embedded inside the .twb file.

Dashboard Interactions & Highlights
  • Filter Quantity By Segment & Order Priority – interactive segregation
  • Filter losses by Market – isolate loss-making zones
  • Filter KPIs By Sub-Category / Country – granular drill-downs
  • Show Country Wikipedia Page – external URL action adds context
  • Highlight 1 (generated) – dynamic highlighting across charts
All 11 dashboards are linked via consistent filter actions, enabling cross-dashboard exploration.
